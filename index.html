How it decides

Read the rules

For each second the page takes the average C/N0 of the 4 strongest satellites and compares it with the normal level for your log (the 75th percentile, or the slider value).

Jamming: severe C/N0 drop and the AGC fell too (the receiver turned its gain down because extra radio power arrived), or no satellites at all while AGC fell.

Interference: degraded C/N0 with an AGC drop, or, on phones without AGC, even the high-elevation satellites are badly weakened.

Blockage: C/N0 dropped but the AGC stayed put. Trees, roofs, buildings, tunnels.

No signal: no satellites and no AGC evidence either way.

Unusual: 6+ satellites all very strong and nearly identical (a spoofing hint, not proof).

Seconds without a GPS position get the phone's Wi-Fi/cell position, or are placed along the route between the known positions before and after (hollow markers).
